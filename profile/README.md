## Poplin

**A US-dollar account for freelancers and businesses to get paid, hold, and send money across
borders — in your own name, controlled only by you.**

A personal account is built for freelancers and remote workers who get paid from abroad.
Poplin Business is built for companies trading across borders.

### Who we are

Poplin is a service of Maash Inc., a financial technology company, not a bank, and it never holds
or has access to your funds. Banking and payment services are provided by our licensed partners:
Lead Bank (US), Banking Circle (EU), ClearBank (UK), and Zand Bank (UAE). Maash Inc. is registered
with FinCEN as a Money Services Business, registration number 31000316638898.

### About this organisation

Poplin's product code is private, so this organisation hosts no public repositories. It exists to
hold the company's GitHub identity and its verified `poplinmoney.com` domain.

To report a security issue, please see [Security &
Compliance](https://poplinmoney.com/security/). For anything else,
[support@poplinmoney.com](mailto:support@poplinmoney.com).

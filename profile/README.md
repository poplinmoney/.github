## Poplin

**A US dollar account for individuals and businesses to get paid, hold, and send money between
countries — from an account in your own name that only you control.**

A personal Poplin account is for freelancers and remote workers who get paid by clients and
platforms. A Poplin business account is for companies that get paid by clients and pay suppliers
and staff.

### Who we are

Poplin is a service of Maash Inc., a financial technology company, not a bank. Poplin can’t spend,
move, or freeze your balance. Banking and payment services are provided by our licensed partners:
Lead Bank and Portage Bank (US), Banking Circle and OpenPayd (EU), ClearBank (UK), and Zand Bank
(UAE). Maash Inc. is registered with FinCEN as a Money Services Business, registration number
31000316638898.

### About this organisation

Poplin’s product code is private. This organisation holds the company’s GitHub identity and its
verified `poplinmoney.com` domain, and one public repository,
[poplinmoney/agents](https://github.com/poplinmoney/agents), which documents Poplin’s MCP servers
for AI assistants.

To report a security issue, please see [Security &
Compliance](https://poplinmoney.com/security/). For anything else,
[support@poplinmoney.com](mailto:support@poplinmoney.com).
